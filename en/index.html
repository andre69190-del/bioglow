Citizen science for habitats

BIOGLOW helps you recognise animals and plants around you, document what you observe and protect habitats – a project by Team Maßbots for the FIRST LEGO League 2026/27.

Open the app → Learn more

Runs right in your browser – on your phone, use “Add to Home Screen” to install it as an app. The app follows your phone’s language (English or German).

🔎

Recognise species

Record observations and identify species – supported by open nature databases.

📸

Document

Capture finds with a photo and location and build up a picture of the habitat on your doorstep.

🌱

Protect

Understand which habitats are valuable – and turn that into concrete action.

What’s inside?

  • 🎯 24 care missions – from a dead-wood corner to a hedgehog gap in the fence. Missions that fit the season are at the top; safety tips are included.
  • 🧺 Before/after proof – for litter actions, a photo before and after, plus the amount and the number of helpers.
  • 📈 Monitoring – fixed photo points with an alignment aid, a soil check (dry/moist/wet) and the number of species, so changes become visible.
  • 🧠 Quiz by age group – nature knowledge, “Who am I?” riddles, “Myth or fact?” and recognising species from photos.
  • 👥 Groups & actions – collect together with a group code and share actions. How it works
  • 🔒 Data-minimal – finds stay on your device first; locations of endangered species are blurred. Privacy policy (in German)

Get started

No installation needed – just open the app. On your phone you can also add it to your home screen and use it offline.
